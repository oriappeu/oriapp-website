# FAQ | Ori, the AI goal coach

## What is Ori?

Ori is an AI goal coach. It turns your goal into a route of milestones and plans the steps into your calendar. When a week goes wrong, it makes the next step smaller or different instead of leaving you a pile of tasks. It's in testing now and opens first in the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

## Is this the same Ori as other apps with that name?

No. Ori from oriapp.eu is made by the two of us, Damian Knoth and Jindřich Novák, in the Czech Republic. It isn't related to WONE's Ori, the ORI weight-loss coach, Ori AI Assistant, the oriapp.ai app or Ori Living furniture.

## What does Ori run on?

Android and iPhone. Our testers already use it on both.

## What language is the app in?

English for now. Czech and Slovak are on the way.

## What does Ori know about me, and what does it do with it?

Only what it needs to plan: your goals, your plan, what you've done and how it went. In the app you can see what Ori knows about you, download your data, and delete it along with your whole account at any time. We don't sell your data and we don't train AI on it.

## Does Ori work with Google Calendar?

Yes, if you want it to. Ori checks when you're busy so it never plans steps over your meetings, and it adds its own steps as events marked [Ori]. It doesn't change your other events. You can disconnect it any time in your profile.

## How much time does it take a day?

Setting up takes about twenty minutes, once. After that, a glance in the morning at what the day holds is enough. The rest of the time belongs to the steps themselves - and Ori sizes them to the time and energy you really have.

## Do I need a smartwatch or sleep tracking?

No. You tell Ori how much energy you have today with one tap: Low, Okay or High. It reads the rest from what you did and what you skipped.

## What kind of goals is Ori for?

Goals that take regular work over weeks or months: passing [a language exam](https://oriapp.eu/en/stay-consistent-learning-a-language), running [a half-marathon](https://oriapp.eu/en/start-running-and-keep-going), starting a side project, learning a new skill. If a goal is still vague ("get fit"), Ori helps you make it concrete. It isn't a replacement for therapy or medical care.

## What if I drop out completely for a week or two?

That's fine - Ori expects it. It notices the silence, reaches out first and rebuilds the plan, with no backlog waiting for you. You carry on from where you are: same goal, different path. How to handle it, with or without an app: [What to do when your plan falls apart](https://oriapp.eu/en/when-your-plan-falls-apart).

## Can I have more than one goal at a time?

Yes. Ori plans them together, so they don't fight over the same time or go beyond your capacity.

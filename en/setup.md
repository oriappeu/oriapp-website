## Choose your phone

You’re on a computer. Open this page on your phone: https://oriapp.eu/en/setup

## Android

If you’re not on the waitlist yet, your email puts you on it.

1. Tap **Download for Android**. The app’s file (.apk) downloads to your phone.

2. Open it from the notification or from your Downloads.

3. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps (Install unknown apps). It asks only the first time.

4. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway. Ori isn’t in Google Play yet, so Google doesn’t know it.

5. Open Ori and create your account. Use the same email you give us for testing, so we can switch on the AI features for you.

Most updates reach the app on their own, without a new file. When a new file is needed, we’ll tell you: install it over the old one and your account and data stay.

## iPhone

1. Open [oriapp-test.vercel.app](https://oriapp-test.vercel.app) in Safari.

2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).

3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

4. Open Ori from the new icon on your home screen, not from Safari, and create your account. Use the same email you give us for testing.

5. When Ori asks to send you notifications, allow them. They work only in the app opened from the icon, on iOS 16.4 or newer.

There’s nothing to download and nothing to update: the app on your home screen is always the latest version.

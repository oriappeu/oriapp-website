## Sounds familiar?

- **On Sunday evening you write the plan for the week.** By Wednesday a meeting runs long and it's over.

- **After work you have no energy left even to decide where to start.** So you open Instagram.

- **One skipped workout.** A week later it is a skipped month.

- **The app waits until you come back to a pile of unfinished tasks.** So you would rather not come back.

- **This is the third time this year you're starting this goal.**

You're not someone who doesn't finish things. Your plan just assumed you'd be the same person every week.

What broke the plan this year: a meeting ran long, illness, exams, friends, visitors, moving house, a new project at work, holiday, kids, nothing in particular.

## When you leave the route, Ori recalculates it.

Ori is an AI goal coach: it breaks your goal into steps in your calendar and recalculates them when a week goes wrong. A typical app moves your task to tomorrow. Tomorrow it is there again, only bigger. Ori does what the navigation in your car does. It notices you are off the route and finds a new one. A smaller step instead of a big one. Saturday instead of Thursday. The same goal.

**It notices on its own.** When you go quiet for a few days, Ori reaches out first. You don't have to report that it isn't going well.

**It shrinks it, or changes it.** It doesn't only change the date. It makes the scope smaller or picks a different activity you can manage today.

**You carry on from where you are.** The same goal, a new route. The pile of unfinished tasks never shows up.

On track, even after today

When you leave the route, Ori recalculates it.

A typical app moves your task to tomorrow. Tomorrow it is there again, only bigger. Ori does what the navigation in your car does. It notices you are off the route and finds a new one. A smaller step instead of a big one. Saturday instead of Thursday. The same goal.

It notices on its own.When you go quiet for a few days, Ori reaches out first. You don't have to report that it isn't going well.

It shrinks it, or changes it.It doesn't only change the date. It makes the scope smaller or picks a different activity you can manage today.

You carry on from where you are.The same goal, a new route. The pile of unfinished tasks never shows up.

Try it.

  1. 1Tap "Low". You have no energy today.
  2. 2Tick off the smaller step Ori suggested.
  3. 3Open the Roadmap. The goal is still on track.

That's what Ori does every day. So you don't have to.

We're building it because we needed it.

We're two students. We've each started side projects before, and we ran into the same thing. The plan was ready in an evening. It fell apart the first week something else came up.

We looked for an app that expects this. None of them fit. When we talked to friends, they said they needed something like it too. So we said: we are doing this.

More people backed us than we expected. We talked the idea through with people from the field and validated it through JIC and Red Bull Basement. Now we are testing Ori with a first group of people.

When does Ori launch, and when do I get access?

Ori is in testing now and there's no exact launch date yet. We'll open access step by step, in the Czech Republic and Slovakia first - people on the waitlist get it among the first.

How much will it cost?

We're still settling the pricing. As soon as it's clear, the waitlist hears first.

What does Ori run on?

Android and iPhone. Our testers already use it on both.

What language is the app in?

English for now. Czech and Slovak are on the way.

How is it different from ChatGPT, Notion or Todoist?

ChatGPT writes you a plan, then stops watching whether it's working. Notion and Todoist are lists you manage yourself. Ori lays the plan into your calendar, notices when it starts to break, and rebuilds it around what you have in you right now - not just a day later, but smaller or different.

What does Ori know about me, and what does it do with it?

Only what it needs to plan: your goals, your plan, what you've done and how it went. In the app you can see what Ori knows about you, download your data, and delete it along with your whole account at any time. We don't sell your data and we don't train AI on it.

How much time does it take a day?

Setting up takes about twenty minutes, once. After that, a glance in the morning at what the day holds is enough. The rest of the time belongs to the steps themselves - and Ori sizes them to the time and energy you really have.

What kind of goals is Ori for?

Goals that take regular work over weeks or months: passing a language exam, running a half-marathon, starting a side project, learning a new skill. If a goal is still vague ("get fit"), Ori helps you make it concrete. It isn't a replacement for therapy or medical care.

What if I drop out completely for a week or two?

That's fine - Ori expects it. It notices the silence, reaches out first and rebuilds the plan, with no backlog waiting for you. You carry on from where you are: same goal, different path.

Can I have more than one goal at a time?

Yes. Ori plans them together, so they don't fight over the same time or go beyond your capacity.

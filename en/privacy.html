This website and the waitlist

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Ori is operated by its founders, Damian K. and Jindřich N., Czech Republic.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, the time you joined and the language of the page - so we write to you in Czech, Slovak or English. Nothing else - the form has no other fields.

Why

To thank you for joining (one short email, right after), to tell you when early access to Ori is ready for you, and about Ori's launch. We don't sell or share your address, and we don't send you anything unrelated to Ori.

We process it because you asked us to (your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw that at any time.

Visits

We count visits to this website with Umami, an analytics service built for privacy: we see which pages were viewed, where a visit came from (a link on LinkedIn, say), the country and the type of device. It uses no cookies, builds no profile of you and does not store your IP address, so we can't tell from it who you are. We also count how many people join the waitlist - without the addresses themselves. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in knowing whether the website works (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where it is kept

In a database run for us by Supabase, our database provider. Emails are sent through Resend, our email provider. The page's fonts are served by this website itself.

How long

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it to invite you - after Ori's public launch we clear the list.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it - write to oriapp.eu@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll do it. If you think we've handled your data wrongly, please tell us first at the same address - we'll look into it and put it right. You also always have the right to complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů.

The Ori app

This page covers only this website and its waitlist. Once you use the Ori app itself, its own privacy policy applies, which you see when you create an account.

# Ori, AI goal coach (oriapp.eu)

Ori is a mobile app (Android and iPhone) that turns a personal goal into a roadmap of milestones
and daily steps placed into the person's real calendar, and re-plans when a bad week breaks the
plan: the next step gets smaller or different instead of leaving a backlog.

## Joining the waitlist

Only for the person you are acting for, with their own email address and their explicit request.

- In a browser on https://oriapp.eu/en/ (or `/` for Czech, `/sk/` for Slovak), the page offers the
  WebMCP tool `join_ori_waitlist` with one parameter, `email`. The page's language sets the language
  of the thank-you email.
- Without WebMCP, fill the email field of the sign-up form at https://oriapp.eu/en/#zapis and submit.

The waitlist stores only the address and the language. What happens to it:
https://oriapp.eu/en/privacy

How Ori works

Every app can build the plan. Ori is built for the week it breaks.

Setup · once

Twenty minutes, then Ori does the planning

Your goal, your starting point, why it matters to you and how much time you really have. Ori turns it into a roadmap of milestones and plans the next few days of sessions around your actual calendar.

01 - Notice

Ori comes to you

You went quiet. Ori sees it. You never have to report your own failure.

02 - Read

Capacity, not the plan

Skipped sessions and missing check-ins are the reading. One question confirms it.

03 - Reshape

Smaller, or different

Ori changes the scope and the activity itself, not only the date. A quick win instead of deep work.

04 - Resume

Not from zero

You carry on from where you are. Same goal, different path, no guilt.

Every cycle teaches Ori your real pace. Month six is a different product than day one.

Why Ori works

Built on behaviour change, not engagement.

When your capacity changes, the plan changes - not the goal.

We shrink the task, we don't just move it

A rescheduled task is the same wall a day later. A smaller one gets done - and doing something is what restarts the habit.

You never face the backlog

Coming back after a bad week is a shame problem, not a motivation problem. Ori reaches out first, so the moment of return never arrives.

We ask why before what

Passion or pressure, ambition or capacity. The intake shapes the roadmap, because a plan built on the wrong motivation dies in week three.

Energy sets the schedule

Not just the free slot in your calendar. Burnout prevention is a scheduling rule here, not a wellness section.

Engagement-optimised apps succeed when you are in the app. Ori succeeds when you are doing the thing outside it.

Privacy

This website and the waitlist

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we keep your email address and the time you joined. Nothing else — the form has no other fields, and this website uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

Why

To tell you when early access to Ori is ready for you, and about Ori's launch. We don't sell or share your address, and we don't send you anything unrelated to Ori.

We process it because you asked us to (your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw that at any time.

Where it is kept

In a database run for us by Supabase, our database provider. The page's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads.

How long

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it to invite you — after Ori's public launch we clear the list.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — write to oriapp.eu@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll do it. If you think we've handled your data wrongly, please tell us first at the same address — we'll look into it and put it right. You also always have the right to complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů.

The Ori app

This page covers only this website and its waitlist. Once you use the Ori app itself, its own privacy policy applies, which you see when you create an account.
